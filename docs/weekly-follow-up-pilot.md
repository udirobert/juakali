# Weekly follow-up pilot

**Status:** planned — no participants recruited yet. The in-app private
workspace (`/workspace/private`) implements the ritual this pilot measures.

## Recruitment

Recruit 5 existing founder–backer pairs who already follow up by phone, WhatsApp, or email. Participation never requires public attribution; consenting to share results for reporting is a separate opt-in. No real funds required.

## Setup (week 0)

Week 0: record the previous week's reminder count and an example of a useful response; confirm the fixed weekly deadline (Friday **18:00 EAT** / 15:00 UTC) and one measure per pair. The product schedules four Fridays starting on explicit acceptance; the ritual itself is industry-agnostic — each pair names their own measure label and unit.

## Weekly ritual (weeks 1–4)

Weeks 1–4: the venture lead records the number (zero counts), what changed, and what help would be useful; the backer responds; the lead confirms whether the action helped. Participation starts **only after the lead explicitly accepts** the invitation — nothing is pre-enrolled, and consent covers membership, not any public record. The private workspace never publishes to the ledger.

## Measurement log

Keep a private log: pair alias, week, report time, first reminder time, requested help, action time, founder-rated usefulness. The manual log and consent trail are the measurement — the app counts check-ins, asks, and responses, but effect claims come from the log, not inferred from app telemetry. Record no sensitive business text in the measurement log.

## Definitions

An unprompted week is a report before the agreed deadline with no preceding reminder / all scheduled pair-weeks (show numerator and denominator). Useful responses are founder-confirmed helpful actions within 7 days / reports asking for help; report no asks separately.

## Week-4 decision

At week 4 the app shows a review screen with counts — four Fridays end, there is no automatic continuation. The facilitator asks both parties separately whether to continue (manual interviews; the app shows counts, it does not conduct the decision). Report pairs where both opted in / enrolled pairs, and withdrawals separately. Review counts and interview objections before adding funding features. Do not infer effectiveness from this small pilot.
